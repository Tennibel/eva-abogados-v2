Background of the firm

We have established ourselves as an innovative alternative for legal services in Mexico, a group of colleagues who have not stopped providing legal and tax services for more than 20 years.

Various companies and a robust client portfolio, who over this time have nobly taught us that our commitment is to client satisfaction, which is why Espinosa, Vasconcelos & Asociados, S.C. is the result of professionally uniting ideas, actions, experiences and work to reach a single goal, to be able to serve efficiently.

Our mission

To establish ourselves as the best firm in the legal and financial field and a leader in corporate matters, providing optimal and accessible services tailored to the specific needs of our users with viable solutions, through specialized, responsible and ethical service. Likewise, to become a means of access to judicial and administrative institutions with the fair application of their regulatory framework in each of them. Providing functionality and security to our users.

Our vision

To grow our prestige, with concrete and innovative solutions, gaining permanent clients and satisfied users, exceeding expectations of quality and service, with responsible and professional staff in all areas, thus maintaining our leadership.

What makes us different

Clear communication and the honesty to set out a work plan and carry it through. We do not create false expectations. We are specialists in our field.

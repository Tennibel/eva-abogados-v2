Technology, Media and Telecommunications (TMT)

We know the regulations applicable to this sector; likewise, we have trained in the related sector in economic competition, consumer protection, advertising, intellectual property, personal data and the regulatory framework of the regulatory authorities in the field of telecommunications, information technologies and the implementation of innovative and disruptive business models.

02

Retail

We know the retail industry because we provide a wide range of specialized services to address the challenges of this sector, including advice on commercial contracts, regulatory compliance, risk management and dispute resolution.

03

Fintech

We have advised various clients from the initial stage of development and design of products and services in their technological, financial and regulatory scope. Drawing on our knowledge of the debate and passage of the Fintech Law, we have achieved its proper application within the real scope of its legal reach.

04

Nearshoring

We understand Mexico’s strategic position on the international stage, which has made it one of the most attractive countries for relocating operations to the North American region; we are therefore committed to advising and assisting our clients in the complex legal field, seeking to strengthen our clients’ presence in our country.

05

E-commerce

We know the regulatory framework and have trained in the dynamic field of digital business, applications, social networks and e-commerce platforms, among others. Our training in intellectual property, industrial property, telecommunications and information technologies allows us to provide comprehensive services in this sector, enhancing the strengths of each of our clients in their specific sector.

06

Healthcare

We are distinguished by extensive experience in the sector, as we trained as advisors in the pharmaceutical sector from the production, distribution and marketing levels, accompanying and assisting our clients, legally and financially, in decision-making and the application of the regulatory framework, managing risks and generating solutions aimed at preventing legal and financial contingencies. Likewise, we know the complexity and dynamism of the private hospital sector, from its commercial interaction with insurers, medical laboratories and users, applying health and commercial regulations.

07

Education

We actively participate in providing strategic and personalized legal solutions in order to consolidate and strengthen businesses in the private education field that guarantee regulatory compliance at the municipal, state and federal levels. All of this from a legal and financial prevention approach in order to mitigate risks.

08

Energy

We participate in advising and assisting energy projects nationwide; specifically, we are characterized by our experience in the development and operation of power, wind, photovoltaic, clean energy, hydrocarbon and service station projects, contract management and compliance with obligations in light of their applicable regulation.

09

Hospitality and tourism

We are identified by our experience in this sector, in which we advise from the initial stage of legal and financial planning for the acquisition, establishment, development, construction and operation of tourism projects for hotels, vacation clubs, private residential clubs, timeshare projects and mixed-use projects.

10

Entertainment and sports

We provide comprehensive advice on the organization, production and execution of sporting, cultural and entertainment events, from the design, drafting, review and negotiation of sponsorship agreements, leasing of sports facilities, advertising, brand activations and commercial collaborations to the hiring of artistic and sports talent and public figures.

11

Real estate

We are defined by our experience in legal and financial analysis for the acquisition of assets, risk management in the leasing and subleasing of real estate, as well as its land-use regulation and use in accordance with urban development plans and the applicable legal framework, defining measurable strategies for capital gain and investment. Likewise, we diagnose, from a legal and financial approach, the most profitable debt or equity financing for your acquisition or lease project.
